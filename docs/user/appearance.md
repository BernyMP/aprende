# Appearance

aprende has one theme, built for long study sessions: parchment pages, ink-dark text and quiet
green accents. Open **Settings → Appearance** to follow the system appearance or stay in light or
dark mode. The choice is saved separately on each device or browser.

Use **Change appearance** in the command palette to choose System, Light, or Dark without leaving
chat. **Cmd+Option+Shift+A** on macOS or **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+A** on Windows/Linux cycles through those
modes. Customize the shortcut under **Settings → Keybindings**.

## Math

Turn on **Render math** in **Settings → Appearance** to typeset LaTeX in messages and Markdown
previews on web, desktop, and mobile. It reads `$…$`, `$$…$$`, `\(…\)`, and `\[…\]`, outside of code.
Fenced `math` blocks also render. Formulas work in tables, including absolute-value bars.
Dollar amounts such as `$5 and $10` stay text; write `\$` to keep any other dollar sign literal.
Wide formulas scroll horizontally while the surrounding text wraps. Copying a formula or
exporting a table preserves the TeX.

The composer keeps TeX source editable, including when rich text is enabled. The Render math
preference is saved separately on each device.

## Motion

The main sidebar, right panel, and terminal drawer open and close immediately. Moving between
threads always snaps to the selected thread's panel state without replaying its transitions.
